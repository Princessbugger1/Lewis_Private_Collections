# Search, Filter, and Visibility Rules

Search and filters are views over the collection, not destructive operations.

- Searching by name, type, country, year, denomination, series, certification number, or other supported field must not alter records.
- Hidden categories, countries, or series remain searchable when the user intentionally searches for them or enables the appropriate visibility option.
- Turning a category off means it is excluded from the normal browse view; it does not delete or archive the underlying records.
- A direct search for a hidden category/item can return those records with a clear indication that they are hidden from the normal browse view.
- Filters can be combined without changing stored data.
- Clearing filters returns the normal collection view.
- Search results should identify the applicable category, country/issuer, and series so an item is not mistaken for a duplicate.
- Search must include user-created classifications and series.
- Search should support unusual/ancient items even when modern series rules do not apply.
- Visibility settings are separate from authentication and sharing permissions.
- A hidden item must remain protected by the same privacy and access controls as a visible item.
