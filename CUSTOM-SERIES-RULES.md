# Custom Series Rules

Users can create their own series at any time. Built-in series are suggestions and do not limit the catalog.

## Create
A user can choose **Add Series / Create New Series**, enter a series name, and optionally provide country/issuer, denomination, date range, description, and notes.

## Use
A custom series can be assigned to any compatible item, searched, filtered, sorted, renamed, hidden, or edited just like a built-in series.

## Safety
- Creating a series never creates duplicate item records.
- Renaming or editing a series does not erase its items.
- Deleting a series, if offered, must remove only the grouping definition; it must not delete the underlying collection items. Items should remain searchable and editable.
- A user-created series can later be changed to a different series or removed from an item without deleting the item.
- Custom series names are preserved in backup/restore.
- Custom series work for U.S., foreign, historical, and unusual items.
