# Specialist Research Links

Lewis Private Collections is the place for your collection records. Specialized references remain external so the main catalog stays simple.

## VAM / Morgan varieties
Use VAM World and other trusted variety references when a Morgan dollar needs detailed attribution. Record the final VAM in the catalog only after you are satisfied with the identification.

## Certification
For slabbed coins, use the grading service's official verification page when available. The catalog stores the service, certification number, grade, and verification/reference link; it does not impersonate or bypass a grading service.

## Design principle
The catalog should suggest and organize information, but uncertain specialist identifications remain marked as **Unknown / Not Determined** until the collector approves the result.
