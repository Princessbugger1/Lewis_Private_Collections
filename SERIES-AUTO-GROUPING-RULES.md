# Series and Automatic Grouping Rules

Series are flexible groupings over item records. The catalog should automatically recognize supported series when enough identification information is confirmed, while always allowing user correction and custom series.

- A series may be associated with classification, country/issuer, denomination, date ranges, design/type, and other identifying rules.
- Adding a coin should automatically place it into the applicable series when identification is sufficiently confident.
- If a year/design combination crosses series boundaries, the system should use the relevant rule rather than relying on year alone.
- Examples include Barber, Mercury, and Roosevelt dimes and U.S. quarter series such as America the Beautiful.
- Series membership is editable and does not change the underlying item identity.
- Users can create a custom series when a desired series is not built in.
- A coin can be viewed through multiple applicable filters without being duplicated.
- Sorting within a series should support chronological order and other useful fields.
- Removing or hiding a series must never delete or alter the member records.
- Automatic series assignment is a suggestion unless the rules produce a sufficiently confirmed match; ambiguous cases remain Needs Review.
