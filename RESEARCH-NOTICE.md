# External Research Notice

Research and identification results may be gathered from outside websites and services. They are provided for research purposes only and may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect.

Always verify the coin or banknote yourself before adding research information to the catalog. Check the date, mint mark, denomination, variety, composition, certification information, and other relevant details against the physical item and/or authoritative references.

Research suggestions are not authentication, grading, appraisal, or proof of ownership.

The catalog should preserve the original photographs even when research cannot find a useful match.

When research information is transferred into a catalog record, the user must review it before saving. The app must not silently overwrite information the user already entered.
