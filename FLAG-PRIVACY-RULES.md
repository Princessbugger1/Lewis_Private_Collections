# Flag Display & Privacy Rules

- Flags are decorative metadata only; they do not establish authenticity, ownership, value, or legal status.
- A flag must correspond to the recorded Country / Issuer when a verified mapping exists.
- Historical issuers may use historical flag assets rather than a modern successor flag.
- Missing or uncertain flag data displays no flag rather than guessing.
- Flag assets must not require sending the user's collection data to an outside service at display time.
- The catalog must not transmit address, precise location, storage location, collection value, private notes, or unrelated inventory data to obtain a flag.
- Users can still enter and group an issuer even when no flag asset exists.
- Changing or hiding flag display must never alter the underlying item record.
