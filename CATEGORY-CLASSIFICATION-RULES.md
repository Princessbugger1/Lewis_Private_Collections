# Category and Classification Rules

Classifications are expandable user-editable metadata, not a fixed enumeration.

- Built-in classifications may include Coin, Token, Counterfeit, Commemorative, Paper Money/Banknote, Medal, Bullion, Exonumia, **Ancient**, and other useful starting values.
- Ancient items are a first-class classification and must not be forced into modern coin series rules.
- Users can add, rename, edit, hide, or retire classifications without deleting the items that use them.
- A classification may be assigned to an item independently of its country/issuer and series.
- Existing records must retain their classification when new built-in classifications are added.
- If a classification is retired, its existing items remain intact and searchable; the user can reclassify them later.
- Classification controls must distinguish Unknown/Not Checked from an explicit No where a yes/no field is used.
- Classification changes must be reversible through normal editing and preserved by backup/restore.
- Classification is never inferred solely from a photograph when evidence is insufficient; automatic suggestions remain reviewable.
