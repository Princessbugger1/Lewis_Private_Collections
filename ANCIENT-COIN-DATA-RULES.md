# Ancient Coin Data Rules

Ancient coins are a first-class catalog area and must remain flexible because ancient attribution often differs from modern country/series classification.

- Ancient is a built-in classification.
- Ancient records may use issuer, ruler/dynasty, culture, region, period, denomination, metal, approximate date range, and attribution notes as applicable.
- Exact year is optional; approximate dates and ranges are valid.
- Country/issuer may be historical and should not be forced into a modern nation-state.
- Users can create custom ancient series/groups for cultures, rulers, dynasties, periods, collections, or personal organization.
- Automatic identification of ancient coins must favor Needs Review/Unknown when evidence is insufficient.
- Provenance, attribution notes, photos, certification/authentication information, and research must remain attached to the stable item record when classification changes.
- Ancient-specific fields must be expandable later without breaking existing modern coin records.
