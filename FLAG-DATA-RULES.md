# Flag Data Rules

Flags are optional visual metadata attached to a country/issuer identity, not to the physical coin image itself.

- A country/issuer may have zero, one, or multiple historically appropriate flag references.
- Historical issuers must be able to use historically appropriate flags without automatically substituting a modern national flag.
- Flag data must be editable independently of the country/issuer name.
- A missing or unavailable flag must never block catalog entry, search, grouping, or backup/restore.
- Flag assets should be referenced by stable identifiers rather than embedding third-party remote content directly into item records.
- The catalog should support adding new flag references without changing existing coin records.
- Flag display is optional and may be disabled in Settings without changing country/issuer data.
- Country/issuer identity remains the authoritative classification; the flag is presentation only.
