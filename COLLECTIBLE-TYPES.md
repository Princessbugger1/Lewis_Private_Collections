# Collectible Types for Research

The research workflow must support more than standard circulating coins.

## Types
- Coins
- Tokens
- Medals / medallic pieces
- Counterfeits / suspected counterfeits
- Other numismatic pieces

## Counterfeit handling
A research result may flag a piece as **possible counterfeit** or **suspected counterfeit**, but an image-only result must never be treated as definitive authentication or a definitive counterfeit determination.

The catalog should preserve the user's original photos and notes and allow the user to record a confirmed or personally determined counterfeit separately from an automated research suggestion.

## Tokens
Token research should be able to identify issuer, location, denomination/value, date or date range, token type, variety, and relevant reference information when available.

All research results remain suggestions until the user explicitly reviews and accepts them.
