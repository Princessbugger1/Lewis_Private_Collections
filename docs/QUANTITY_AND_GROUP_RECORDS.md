# Quantity and Group Records

## Goal
Allow one catalog entry to represent multiple examples of the same collectible when the owner wants to manage them together.

## Quantity field
Each appropriate record should have a small, easy-to-use **Quantity** field. The default quantity is 1.

The owner can enter a whole-number quantity such as 2, 5, or 20.

## When quantity is useful
Examples include:
- multiple identical coins
- rolls or groups of common coins
- duplicate paper-money notes that the owner does not need to catalog separately
- bulk or lot acquisitions

## Individual records remain available
If individual examples later need separate photographs, grades, certification numbers, provenance, or values, the group should be splittable into individual records without losing the original group history.

## Important distinction
Quantity must not be used to hide meaningful differences. If coins have different grades, varieties, certifications, photographs, or other important identifying information, they should be separate records or individually tracked within the group.

## Inventory behavior
Reports and collection totals should account for quantity. A group with Quantity = 10 counts as ten physical pieces while remaining one catalog record.

## Editing
Changing quantity should be a simple edit and should not change the collectible's stable record ID. The system should preserve history when quantity changes because pieces are added, removed, sold, gifted, or otherwise transferred.

## Future-proofing
The quantity model should support later expansion into individual-item tracking without requiring the owner to recreate the collection from scratch.
