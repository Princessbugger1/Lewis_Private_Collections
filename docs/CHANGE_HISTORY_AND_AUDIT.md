# Change History and Audit

## Goal
Keep important record changes understandable without making normal catalog use complicated.

## What to track
Where practical, record important changes such as:
- quantity changes
- archive/unarchive actions
- major edits to identification information
- merges or splits of records
- important attachment changes

## Simple presentation
History should be available from the individual record and should not clutter the main catalog list.

## Protection
Change history should not be casually editable or deleted from the normal record-edit screen.

## No false certainty
The history should distinguish a confirmed change from an Unknown / Not Checked field value.

## Future-proofing
History entries should reference the stable collectible record ID and include enough information to understand what changed without requiring the old record layout.
