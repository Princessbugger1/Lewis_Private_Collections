# Trash, History, and Collection Status

## Active collection
A coin normally appears in the active collection.

## Sold / Transferred
When a coin leaves the collection, the preferred action is to mark it Sold or Transferred rather than deleting its record. Historical details, notes, photos, purchase information, and provenance can remain available.

## Recently Deleted
A deliberate Delete action moves the record to a recoverable trash area rather than immediately destroying it.

Trash actions:
- Restore
- Delete Permanently (with confirmation)
- Empty Trash (with strong confirmation)

## Safety
- Empty Trash is irreversible.
- Deleting one record must never affect another record.
- Historical records must remain separate from active collection counts.
- Future retention policies must never silently destroy customer data merely because a trial or subscription expires.
