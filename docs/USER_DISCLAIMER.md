## Purpose
The catalog is an organizational and research tool for the owner's collection. Identification, grading, valuation, authenticity, signature recognition, rarity, and other research results may contain errors and should be independently verified when important.

## User responsibility
The owner should review important entries before relying on them for purchase, sale, insurance, tax, appraisal, legal, or other consequential decisions.

## Privacy and security
The application should use reasonable safeguards appropriate to its design and deployment, but no software or internet-connected service can promise absolute security. The application should minimize exposure of private collection information and should not claim protection from every possible failure, unauthorized access, breach, data leak, or transmission error.

## Backups and recovery
Users should regularly download/export their collection and keep an independent local backup. Deleted information may be recoverable for a limited period or from an available backup, but recovery is not guaranteed after permanent deletion, expiration, loss, or corruption of all recoverable copies. Users should not rely on recovery as a substitute for maintaining their own backup.

## Sharing
The owner should intentionally review what is shared before publishing or exporting collection records. Private information should not be included in a public catalog unless the owner chooses to share it.
