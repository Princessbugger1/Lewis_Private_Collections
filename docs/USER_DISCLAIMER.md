# Catalog Disclaimer and Risk Notice

## Purpose
The catalog is an organizational and research tool for the owner's collection. Identification, grading, valuation, authenticity, signature recognition, rarity, and other research results may contain errors and should be independently verified when important.

## No guarantee
Information supplied by the catalog, automated tools, external references, or research services is not a guarantee of authenticity, grade, market value, provenance, or completeness.

## User responsibility
The owner should review important entries before relying on them for purchase, sale, insurance, tax, appraisal, legal, or other consequential decisions.

## Privacy and security
The owner should understand where the catalog is hosted and where backups or shared records are stored. No software can promise absolute security. The application should minimize exposure of private collection information and should not claim that information is protected from every possible failure, unauthorized access, or transmission error.

## Sharing
The owner should intentionally review what is shared before publishing or exporting collection records. Private information should not be included in a public catalog unless the owner chooses to share it.
