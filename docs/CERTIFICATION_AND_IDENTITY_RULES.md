# Certification and Identity Rules

## Goal
Keep certification information useful for both single items and grouped quantities without losing the distinction between a group and individually identified pieces.

## Certification fields
A record may include:
- grading service
- grade
- certification number
- designation/variety attribution

Certification information should be optional because many collection items are uncertified.

## Quantity interaction
A group with Quantity greater than 1 may be used when the pieces are genuinely interchangeable for the owner's catalog purposes. If individual pieces have different certification numbers, grades, varieties, or other identifying information, they should be split into individual records or individually tracked.

## No invented certification
The catalog must never generate or infer a certification number. A number entered by the owner or obtained from a research/verification service should remain clearly identified by its source and verification status.

## Search
Certification number and grading service should be searchable independently so the owner can quickly locate a specific certified item.

## Future-proofing
Certification records should remain attached to the stable collectible record ID and support later individualization when a grouped record is split.
