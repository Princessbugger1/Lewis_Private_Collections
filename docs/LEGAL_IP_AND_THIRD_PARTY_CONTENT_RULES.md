# Legal, IP, and Third-Party Content Rules

## Goal
Keep the product's original identity while avoiding accidental copying or misuse of third-party material.

## Original product assets
Create from scratch whenever practical:
- Mascot characters and character sheets.
- Logos, icons, decorative graphics, animations, and sounds.
- UI artwork and illustrations.
- Onboarding dialogue and instructional copy.
- Screensaver scenes and character interactions.

Do not trace, redraw, or closely imitate a competitor's distinctive character or artwork.

## Common functionality
General functions such as cataloging coins, taking photos, searching by year, recording grades, exporting data, or identifying a coin are not treated as proprietary merely because another app also provides them. The implementation, artwork, wording, branding, and other protected expression must still be independently created.

## Third-party references
- Use official APIs, links, or other permitted access methods where available.
- Do not scrape or reproduce protected databases or websites unless permission/terms allow it.
- Link users to authoritative grading/reference services when appropriate rather than copying their protected pages or imagery.
- Respect third-party trademarks and avoid implying endorsement or affiliation.
- User-uploaded third-party material should remain the user's responsibility subject to the product's final Terms and policies.

## Competitive originality checks
Before major commercial release, periodically review comparable coin apps and services for distinctive visual identity, branding, copy, and unusual product presentation. The purpose is to detect potential confusion early, not to copy competitors.

If a feature appears common across many products, treat it as a normal category function and implement an independently designed version. If a particular visual, character, name, phrase, or presentation appears unusually distinctive, deliberately design a materially different version and seek legal advice when appropriate.

## Mascot policy
The mascot family should be original to this product. Candidate names and designs should be screened for confusingly similar existing trademarks/characters before commercial launch.

## Legal review
Before public commercial launch, have qualified counsel review at minimum:
- Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy.
- Data-loss/security disclaimers.
- Subscription and cancellation language.
- User-content provisions.
- Trademark/brand clearance.
- Any patent strategy or patentability questions.
- Third-party data/API licenses and attribution requirements.

This document is a product-development rule set, not legal advice.
