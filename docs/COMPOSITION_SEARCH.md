# Composition Search

## Goal
Allow the owner to quickly find collectibles by metal or other composition.

## Searchable composition
Composition should be a structured, searchable field on applicable records. Examples include:
- silver
- copper
- gold
- nickel
- bronze
- brass
- clad
- steel
- aluminum
- mixed/other

## Free-text support
The owner should also be able to search composition text when a more specific description is needed, while structured composition remains available for reliable filtering.

## Combined searches
Composition should work together with other filters. Examples:
- all silver coins
- copper coins from a particular country
- gold coins from a date range
- silver coins with a specific denomination
- copper items that are still marked Unknown / Not Checked for a particular field

## Paper money
Composition search should apply where material information is meaningful, but the field should not be forced onto paper-money records when it is irrelevant.

## Verification
Composition can be owner-entered, researched, or test-supported. A research or test result should not silently overwrite an owner-confirmed value.

## Future-proofing
The structured composition list should be expandable for unusual alloys and specialized collectibles without changing existing records.
