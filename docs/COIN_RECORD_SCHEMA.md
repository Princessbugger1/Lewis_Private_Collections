# Coin Record Schema

## Goal
Give coins a structured master record that supports identification, research, certification, photographs, and collection management without forcing every field to be completed.

## Core identification
A coin record should support:
- category/type
- country or issuing authority
- denomination
- date/year
- mint mark
- series/type designation
- variety/error
- composition
- weight
- diameter and other known specifications

## Condition and grading
Support raw/uncertified condition notes as well as certified information, including:
- grading service
- grade
- certification number
- designation or variety attribution

## Testing and verification
Where applicable, records may include metal-testing information, specific test method, test status, and notes. Test results should remain distinguishable from assumptions or unverified observations.

## Three-state fields
Applicable yes/no questions should use the master three-state pattern:
- ❓ Unknown / Not Checked
- ✅ Yes
- ❌ No

One tap cycles through the states and another tap continues the cycle back to Unknown. Examples include COA, OGP, original box, Sigma tested, photos complete, and variety checked.

## Photographs
Support owner photographs for obverse, reverse, edge, and detail views. Reference images remain separate and unobtrusive.

## Provenance and valuation
Coin records may link to acquisition/provenance information and separate dated valuation observations without overwriting purchase price or realized sale price.

## Future-proofing
The schema should allow additional coin-specific attributes to be added without changing the identity of the collectible or requiring a separate catalog.
