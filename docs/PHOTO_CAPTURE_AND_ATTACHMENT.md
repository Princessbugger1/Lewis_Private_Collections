# Photo Capture and Attachment

## Goal
Make owner photographs quick to add from a phone while keeping them clearly associated with the correct collectible.

## Owner photos
A collectible should support practical owner-photo slots such as:
- coin obverse/front
- coin reverse/back
- coin edge
- detail photo
- paper-money face
- paper-money back
- certificate/COA photo

## Reference images
External/reference images must remain visually distinct from owner photographs. A small reference thumbnail may be shown unobtrusively, and tapping it can open the reference image. The owner photo remains the authoritative visual record of the owner's item.

## Capture flow
From a phone, the owner should be able to take a photograph or select one from the device and attach it to the current collectible without unnecessary steps.

## Correct-record protection
The interface should clearly identify the collectible before saving an image and should provide a simple way to remove or replace an image without deleting the collectible itself.

## Image quality
The system should preserve an appropriate original image while using smaller thumbnails/previews for fast browsing. It should avoid requiring the owner to manually resize every photograph.

## Metadata
Photos should remain associated with the stable collectible record ID and should identify their role (front, back, edge, detail, COA, etc.).

## Privacy
Owner photographs are private collection data by default. They should not be included in public reports or shared records unless the owner intentionally selects them.

## Future-proofing
Additional photo roles should be addable later without changing the collectible's identity or breaking existing images.
