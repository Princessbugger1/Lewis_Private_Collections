# Photo-Assisted Coin Lookup Workflow

## Goal
Provide a guided way to photograph a coin and research what it may be while keeping identification suggestions separate from confirmed catalog data.

## Required photos
- Obverse — required for a complete lookup
- Reverse — required for a complete lookup
- Edge — optional, but recommended when edge lettering, reeding, shape, or composition may help identify the issue

## Workflow
1. User captures or selects the coin photographs.
2. The app checks that the available views are clear enough for research.
3. A lookup service/research process analyzes the images and available catalog information.
4. The app presents possible matches and supporting information.
5. The user confirms, edits, or rejects the proposed identification.
6. Only confirmed information is written into the master record.

## Research details
Possible results may include:
- country/issuer
- denomination
- date
- mint mark
- type/design
- variety or error possibilities
- composition
- approximate rarity indicators
- grading/certification research leads
- reference images and source information

## Reference image handling
A research/reference image is displayed as a reference only and is never stored as the owner's coin photograph. The user's own photos remain the primary item images.

## Edge cases
The workflow should allow "Unable to identify" or "Needs more information" rather than forcing a guess. Multiple candidate matches may be shown when the photographs do not uniquely identify the coin.

## Future-proofing
The lookup provider should be replaceable. Confirmed catalog data must remain usable if the external identification service changes or becomes unavailable.
