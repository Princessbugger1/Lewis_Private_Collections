# Dark Mode Photo Inspection

## Purpose
Coin photography is one of the most important parts of the catalog, so Dark / Blackout mode must not make coin details harder to inspect.

## Requirements
- Full-size coin photos must remain accurately viewable in both Light and Dark modes.
- The photo viewer should avoid applying a theme filter to the photograph itself.
- UI controls around the photo may adapt to the selected theme, but the coin image must retain its original color and contrast.
- Zoom and pan controls must remain accessible in both themes.
- Captions, certification information, and image metadata must remain readable without obscuring the coin.
- Thumbnail borders/backgrounds should provide enough separation to distinguish the photo from the surrounding interface.

## Testing
Compare the same coin photographs in Light and Dark modes and verify that the interface changes while the photograph's underlying appearance does not.
