# Settings Behavior

## Phone-first design
Settings are designed for the phone first, while remaining usable on tablets and laptops.

## Optional feature switches
Each optional catalog section has its own simple ON/OFF control in Settings.

Examples include COA / Certificate of Authenticity, History & Provenance, photos, composition, weight, and metal testing.

## Hiding is not deleting
Turning a section OFF only hides it from the normal entry and record view. Saved information remains stored and can be shown again by turning the section ON.

## Simple wording
Use plain labels such as ON and OFF rather than technical terms such as modular.

## Safe changes
Changing a setting must not alter the underlying collectible data.
