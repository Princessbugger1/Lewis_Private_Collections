# Item History and Provenance

## Goal
Keep useful history with each collectible without making normal entry complicated.

## Optional information
A record may include:
- where the item came from
- purchase/source notes
- previous owner or provenance notes when appropriate
- acquisition date when the owner wants to record it
- important history notes

## Simple entry
These fields should remain optional and should not clutter the main identification section.

## Record history
Important changes to a collectible should be traceable, especially quantity changes, merges/splits, and transfers.

## Privacy
Provenance and source information is private collection information by default.

## Future-proofing
History should remain attached to the stable collectible record ID so the record can evolve without losing its background.
