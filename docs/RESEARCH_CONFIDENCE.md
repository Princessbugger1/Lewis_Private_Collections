# Research Confidence and Verification

## Purpose
Research tools can be useful without being treated as infallible. The catalog should make it obvious what is confirmed by the owner and what is only a research suggestion.

## Confidence states
Research results may be represented as:
- Suggested — generated or found by research, not yet confirmed
- Confirmed — owner accepted or entered the information
- Corrected — owner changed a suggested result
- Rejected — owner determined the suggestion does not apply
- Needs more information — available evidence is insufficient

## Display
Suggested information should have a clear but uncluttered indication that it is not yet confirmed. Confirmed catalog information should be visually distinct.

## Auditability
Where practical, retain the research source and date for important conclusions such as identification, rarity, variety, signatures, or valuation.

## No forced answers
The app must allow the user to leave a field Unknown / Not Checked or mark a lookup as Unable to identify. It should never require a guess merely to complete a record.

## Future-proofing
Confidence and verification metadata belongs to the master record so simple views can hide it while advanced research views can show it.
