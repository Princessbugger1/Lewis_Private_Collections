# Valuation and Research Plan

## Purpose
The catalog should help research a collectible without presenting an automated estimate as a guaranteed market value.

## Research information
A future lookup workflow may gather:
- identification
- comparable examples
- grade/condition considerations
- known varieties and errors
- rarity indicators
- auction or sales references
- certification information
- relevant historical/background information

## Value fields
Keep these distinct:
- purchase price
- owner's estimated value
- researched market range
- valuation date
- valuation notes
- source/reference information

## Accuracy rule
Research and automated suggestions are informational. The catalog should identify uncertainty and preserve the source or evidence used for a conclusion where practical.

## Photo-assisted lookup
The user's own photographs should be the primary input for identification. Coins should support obverse, reverse, and optional edge images. Paper money should support face and reverse images.

## Future-proofing
The research provider should be replaceable. Saved catalog records must remain useful if an external lookup service changes or becomes unavailable.
