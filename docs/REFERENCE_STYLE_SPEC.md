# Reference-Style Catalog Presentation

The catalog should feel familiar to collectors who use printed numismatic references while remaining a personal digital collection system.

## Presentation principles
- Use a clean, structured list with predictable fields.
- Prioritize year, denomination, country/mint, and condition/grade for quick recognition.
- Avoid copying proprietary Red Book text, pricing tables, or copyrighted material.
- Collector-entered data and personal photos remain the user's own catalog information.
- Tapping a coin opens the richer digital record rather than forcing every field into the list.

## Digital advantages
The app can provide capabilities a printed reference cannot: personal photographs, measured weight, testing observations, purchase records, custom groups, history, search, filters, and editable notes.

The goal is "reference-book familiarity without reference-book clutter."
