# Collection Hierarchy Specification

## Top-level collection areas
Use a broad top-level navigation label: **Currency & Collectibles**.

Under it, keep familiar, plain-language categories:
- Coins
- Paper Money
- Commemorative Coins
- Medals
- Tokens
- Other Collectibles

## UX principle
The broad heading provides room for future collectible types, while the individual category names remain simple and immediately understandable.

## Search and filters
Users can search across the entire collection or restrict results to one category. Category selection should not erase search text or unrelated saved records.
