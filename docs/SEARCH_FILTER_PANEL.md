# Search and Filter Panel

## Goal
Provide fast ways to find records without cluttering the main catalog screen.

## Search
A prominent search control should support common terms such as item name, denomination, country, date, certification number, and composition.

## Filters
Advanced filters should be available in a separate expandable/filter area rather than taking over the main screen.

## Composition
Composition is a structured filter, allowing searches such as Silver, Copper, Gold, and other recorded materials.

## Status filters
Three-state fields must preserve their three distinct meanings when filtered:
- ❓ Unknown / Not Checked
- ✅ Yes
- ❌ No

Unknown must never be grouped with No.

## Optional sections
Filters for optional sections, such as COA or History & Provenance, should remain available even when those sections are hidden from the normal record-entry view.

## Combined filters
Filters should work together and show the matching records clearly. Applying filters must never modify collection data.

## Mobile layout
The filter panel should be phone-friendly, with simple controls and clear Apply/Clear actions.
