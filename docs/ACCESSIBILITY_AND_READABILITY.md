# Accessibility and Readability

## Goal
Make the catalog comfortable to use on a phone and easy to understand at a glance.

## Touch targets
Buttons, toggles, search controls, and three-state fields should be large enough for reliable tapping.

## Labels
Important actions and fields should use plain, unambiguous wording.

## Status visibility
The three-state status must be visually distinct and understandable from the symbol and label, not color alone.

## Optional sections
Hidden optional sections should not leave confusing empty space or broken layouts.

## Text and photos
Text should remain readable without unnecessary crowding, and photos should be viewable clearly when opened.

## Confirmation actions
Important actions such as delete, restore backup, and other collection-wide changes should have clearly separated Cancel and Confirm choices.

## Future-proofing
The layout should remain usable as the collection grows and as more optional sections are added.
