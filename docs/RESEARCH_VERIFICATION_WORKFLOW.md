# Research and Verification Workflow

## Goal
Make automated and external research useful while keeping the owner in control of what becomes confirmed catalog information.

## Research result states
A researched result should be distinguishable as:
- Not researched
- Research suggested
- Owner confirmed
- Owner corrected
- Needs further verification

## Suggested information
Research may propose an identification, date, mint mark, variety, grade guidance, comparable sale, or other information. A suggestion must not silently replace existing owner-confirmed information.

## Sources
Where practical, retain the source and date associated with researched information. Reference images should retain source information when available.

## Conflicting results
If multiple sources disagree, the catalog should show the conflict rather than silently selecting one answer. The owner can review the evidence and make the final catalog decision.

## Images and recognition
Image recognition may assist with identification, but it should be treated as research assistance rather than proof of authenticity, grade, or value.

## Manual correction
The owner must be able to correct a research result without losing the fact that an automated or external suggestion existed.

## Privacy
Private collection information should not be sent to an external research service unless the owner has intentionally enabled the relevant feature and understands what information is being shared.

## Future-proofing
Research providers should be replaceable. Confirmed catalog information must remain usable if a particular provider, API, or website becomes unavailable.
