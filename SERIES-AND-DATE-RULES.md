# Series and Date Rules

Series are expandable, editable groupings derived from record data and user choices. They must not be limited to a fixed list.

- A user can add a new series at any time.
- Series names are editable without changing the stable record ID.
- An item can be moved between series without creating a duplicate.
- Existing series remain available even if no current record is assigned to them.
- A series can be hidden from normal browsing without deleting its records.
- Year/date information is stored independently from the displayed series name.
- Where a recognized series can be determined from date, denomination, mint, issuer, and other applicable attributes, the catalog may suggest the series automatically.
- Automatic series suggestions are editable and must remain proposals until accepted when ambiguity exists.
- Historical and ancient items must not be forced into modern date-based series rules.
- Date ranges and series boundaries must be maintained as rules/data rather than hard-coded into individual records, so future corrections can be made without rewriting the collection.
- For U.S. coinage, examples such as Barber, Mercury, and Roosevelt dimes may be suggested from the relevant date/type rules, but the user can correct the classification when a particular item requires a different identification.
- Specialized sets and commemorative series can be added without requiring a software update.
