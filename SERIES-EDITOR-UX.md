# Series Editor UX

Provide a clear **Add Series / Create New Series** action from the series selector and from Settings.

A series editor should support:
- Series name (required)
- Country / Issuer (optional)
- Denomination (optional)
- Start/end years (optional)
- Description and notes (optional)

The user must be able to edit a custom series later.

Assigning an item to a custom series must not copy the item. The item keeps one stable record ID.

If a series is deleted, only the series definition/group is removed; assigned items remain in the collection and become unassigned/searchable. No item photos, notes, values, certification, provenance, or research may be deleted as a side effect.

The series selector should distinguish built-in suggestions from user-created series, while allowing both to be edited/overridden at the item level.
