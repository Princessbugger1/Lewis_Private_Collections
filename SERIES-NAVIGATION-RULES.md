# Series Navigation Rules

Series should be easy to browse while remaining flexible enough for a growing collection.

- The user can open a series to see all records assigned to it.
- Records within a series should default to chronological order by year/date when a valid date exists, with a clear option for another sort order.
- Unknown dates remain grouped without inventing a year.
- A series can contain records with different grades, mints, varieties, or certification states.
- Series navigation must not require every record to have a series; unclassified or Other records remain accessible.
- Adding a new item to an existing series should be available directly from that series view.
- A user-created series can be expanded with additional items later.
- Series names, descriptions, and ordering are editable.
- Removing a record from a series removes only the grouping relationship; it does not delete the record.
- Search and filters may narrow a series view without changing its stored membership.
- Series views must work on both phone and desktop and should not hide the Add Item or Edit controls.
